# League Rules

**Format: best single lap**
- Each race day has one track. Drivers run it one at a time.
- Unlimited warm-up, then 10 attempts. Your fastest valid lap is your time.
- Penalties are added as seconds to that lap.
- DNF = no valid lap in 10 attempts (0 points).
- Equal lap times: the driver who ran earlier wins.

**Points** (stored per season, so they can change)
- 1st–8th: 10 · 8 · 6 · 5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 1. 9th and below: 0. No bonus points.

**Season tie-break**
- Points, then wins, then 2nds, then 3rds. Still level = shared position.

**Running order**
- Very first race: random.
- After that: from current standings, leader runs last; last place runs first.
- Drivers with no standings yet run first, in random order.
- First race of a new season uses the previous season's final standings.

**Rookies**
- A driver's first season is stored permanently and shows a Rookie badge during that season.
